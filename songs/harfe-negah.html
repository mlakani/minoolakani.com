About the song

Harfe Negah by Minoo Lakani

This official page presents حرف نگاه through its original Persian title, Latin transliteration, English meaning, cover artwork, and complete music video. The dedicated URL gives the song a clear identity within Minoo Lakani’s searchable music catalog.

A look can communicate affection, distance, hesitation, or truth without spoken language. The title recognizes that silent exchange and gives emotional importance to what is understood between two people through their eyes.
