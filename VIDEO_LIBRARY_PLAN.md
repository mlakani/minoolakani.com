# Video Library Plan

This document records the approved format and expansion rules for the official video page at https://minoolakani.com/videos.html.

## Current approved format

- Preserve the existing black, brown, cream, and gold Minoo Lakani Production design.
- Keep responsive 16:9 YouTube players.
- Every video card includes:
  - Official song title
  - Language and content label
  - Short description
  - Embedded YouTube player
  - Direct "Watch on YouTube" link
- Keep the official channel button connected to https://www.youtube.com/@minoolakanimusic.
- Preserve mobile-friendly responsive behavior.
- Do not add overlays, watermarks, or unrelated branding to the source videos.

## Rules when adding more videos

- Keep the current presentation while the catalog remains small.
- Feature three priority videos prominently at the top.
- Separate or filter Persian and English videos as the catalog grows.
- Prefer lightweight thumbnail cards for the larger archive; load a YouTube player only after a visitor clicks.
- At approximately 10–15 videos, convert the page into a structured video library.
- Display about 12–20 videos at a time and use pagination or a "Load More" control.
- Add filters for language, year, song type, and newest releases when useful.
- Organize matching playlists on the official YouTube channel.
- Give important songs dedicated pages with artwork, lyrics, video, and streaming links.
- Optimize for fast loading, accessibility, mobile use, and search engines.
- Do not place hundreds of active iframe players on one page.

## Long-term catalog goal

The website should be able to support hundreds of songs and videos without becoming slow or difficult to navigate. YouTube remains the video host; minoolakani.com serves as the organized official catalog and presentation layer.
