About Minoo Lakani

Minoo Lakani is an independent songwriter and producer creating Persian and English songs about love, loss, hope, and belonging.

Creative Profile

Music from memory, emotion, and lived experience

She blends pop and rock with Persian melodic influences and cinematic arrangements. Piano, expressive guitars, strings, and flute bring warmth and movement to her storytelling.

Her songs explore the closeness of family, the ache of separation, and the courage to begin again. Some celebrate shared moments and lasting love; others express a deep connection to Iran, its people, and the longing for home.

Through lyrics, melody, and arrangement, Minoo lets each story guide the music—from intimate acoustic passages to sweeping orchestral moments. Persian musical traditions bring depth and color to a contemporary sound, connecting the two languages in which she creates.

At the heart of her work is a desire to make listeners feel something: the comfort of a familiar memory, the excitement of a reunion, or the strength found in hope.

Artistic Direction

A bilingual catalog with a unified identity

Persian songwriting

Persian-language releases preserve the emotional meaning of the original titles while also using Latin transliterations and English title meanings to support international discovery.

English releases

English-language songs extend the same personal storytelling into direct, memorable phrases designed to connect across audiences and platforms.

Independent production

Song concepts, production choices, artwork, catalog organization, and official release pages are developed as parts of one consistent artist identity.
