Official Biography

Music That Speaks From the Heart

Minoo Lakani creates contemporary music that brings intimate storytelling together with elegant, cinematic atmosphere. Her work moves between Persian and English, giving each song its own emotional world while maintaining a recognizable artistic identity.

As an independent artist, songwriter, and producer, she develops each release as a complete experience—from the lyrical concept and musical direction to the visual presentation and official release strategy.

Her artistic focus is direct and timeless: meaningful stories, memorable melodies, and honest emotion.
